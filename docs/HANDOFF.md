## Creative direction: APPROVED (round 2, 2026-11-27)
The user's process was: `creative-director` ↔ `design-critic` (`.claude/agents/`) iterate until both are satisfied.
Round 1 got REVISE, round 2 got APPROVE (see `design/critique.md`).

- **References:** `design/references/` (4 pixel-art lab videos + stills/contact sheets).
- **Direction:** `design/direction.md` (includes the "Round 2 changes" section). Type is Silkscreen (multiples of 8px)
  plus Atkinson Hyperlegible for symbols, formulas and small numbers. Pixelify Sans was rejected for C/O and 5/S confusion.
  Public screens never reveal catalysts or recipes during play (they show a sealed "?" flask).
- **Mockups:** `design/screens/*.html` share `kit.js`/`kit.css`; `type-test.html` is the glyph and tier test sheet.
  Screenshots via `bash scripts/shoot-mockups.sh` → `design/screens/shots/`.
- **Organizer decisions (see direction.md §11):** Day Shift manual only; names public unless the player opts out;
  isotope posts may hint a rough area; lab number comes from the college ID digits.

## Design implemented and live (2026-11-27, commits a2079fe..)
- Theme = `design/tokens.css` in `src/app/globals.css` (+ panel/btn/`pf-16..64` pixel-font classes; `pf` is NOT padding).

- DB: migrations 6 to 8 are applied to production. They add `players.name_public` and `_lab_no` / `_public_name`, mask
  opted-out names in feed, leaderboard, hint book and finale, add the `set_name_public` RPC, and keep feed lines in sync with
  renames using an anchored prefix swap. Names in private 1:1 exchanges (lender, code user, gift parties, alliance) stay real.
- Tests: `npm run test:sql` (now including opt-out, rename and forgery cases), `npm test` (33, including `tests/unit/design.test.ts`).
